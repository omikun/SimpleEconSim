## Detailed Thematic Breakdowns

### 1. Modern Economic Warfare & Financial Statecraft

These titles treat supply lines, clearing networks, credit ratings, and central bank decrees as the high-tech artillery of modern geopolitical conflict.

#### 1.1 _The Economic Weapon_
* **Inspiration:** Nicholas Mulder's acclaimed history *The Economic Weapon: The Rise of Sanctions as a Tool of Modern War*.
* **Gameplay Connection:** Captures non-kinetic siege warfare. When a rival power enacts a naval blockade, your maritime nitrate imports drop from +3t to zero, plunging soil fertility below 0.65 and triggering a Turnip Winter famine without a single trench battle.
* **Tagline:** *"Starvation without a shot fired."*

#### 1.2 _Call of Capital: Modern Economic Warfare_
* **Inspiration:** *Call of Duty: Modern Warfare*.
* **Gameplay Connection:** Satirical, memorable, and immediately recognizable. Reframes "operators" as central bankers, finance ministers, and customs commanders. Weapons of choice: mark-to-market write-downs, Tommy shop company scrip, and deposit freezes.
* **Tagline:** *"The frontline is the exchange rate. The casualty is the currency."*

#### 1.3 _Weaponized Interdependence_
* **Inspiration:** Henry Farrell and Abraham Newman's foundational concept in international relations.
* **Gameplay Connection:** Highlights how open trade networks are a trap: specialization makes an economy vulnerable to maritime chokepoints and international banking freezes.
* **Tagline:** *"Trade binds the world. Power pulls the cord."*

#### 1.4 _World in Default_
* **Inspiration:** *World in Conflict* (Massive Entertainment).
* **Gameplay Connection:** Focuses on the catastrophic domino effect when sovereign bonds fail: domestic commercial banks write down capital, trigger Corralito cash-rationing, and force emergency depositor bail-in haircuts.
* **Tagline:** *"When empires can no longer conquer territory, they conquer credit."*

#### 1.5 _DEFCON: Sovereign Shock_
* **Inspiration:** Introversion Software's *DEFCON: Everybody Dies*.
* **Gameplay Connection:** Escalation levels are measured by macroeconomic severity rather than nuclear silos:
  1. *Tariffs & Trade Barriers*
  2. *Company Scrip & Peonage*
  3. *Naval Blockades & Soil Depletion*
  4. *Corralito Deposit Freezes*
  5. *General Strike, Famine & Class Rebellion*

#### 1.6 _The Shock Doctrine: Total War_
* **Inspiration:** Naomi Klein's *The Shock Doctrine*.
* **Gameplay Connection:** The ruling class exploits manufactured or real crises (harvest failure, bank runs) to pass statutory decrees like the Anti-Truck Act repeal or emergency wealth confiscation (bail-ins).

### 2. Famous 4X / Strategy Classics with an Anti-Capitalist Twist

These titles subvert beloved strategy franchises by swapping imperialist triumphalism for historical materialism and class struggle.

#### 2.1 _Enclosure Universalis_
* **Inspiration:** *Europa Universalis* (Paradox Interactive).
* **Gameplay Connection:** Enclosing common lands drives peasants into debt-bonded wage labor, sparking multi-turn fence-tearing rebellions with organization, announcement, march, protest, and violent confrontation.
* **Tagline:** *"Every fence has two sides: property and revolt."*

#### 2.2 _Imperialism: The Highest Stage_
* **Inspiration:** SSI's classic *Imperialism* (1997) and V.I. Lenin's *Imperialism: The Highest Stage of Capitalism*.
* **Gameplay Connection:** Matches the late-game dynamic: domestic markets reach saturation, the rate of profit falls, and nations must export capital, enforce foreign debt at gunpoint, and seize overseas mineral nitrate ports.
* **Tagline:** *"Expand the market. Export the surplus. Enforce the coupon."*

#### 2.3 _Base & Superstructure_
* **Inspiration:** Karl Marx's materialist formulation of society.
* **Gameplay Connection:** The economic base (zero-leak double-entry ledger, goods circulation, physical nitrate stores) strictly dictates the political superstructure (regimes, decrees, elections, police actions). No decree can violate physical accounting.
* **Tagline:** *"You govern the laws. The ledger governs you."*

#### 2.4 _Class War: Total War_
* **Inspiration:** Creative Assembly's *Total War* franchise.
* **Gameplay Connection:** Traditional 4X games treat populations as uniform productivity counters. Here, society is split: mine owners paying in scrip, workers buying at 35% markups in Tommy shops, radical veterans leading street barricades, and soup kitchens funding strikers.

#### 2.5 _Dialectical Civilization_
* **Inspiration:** Sid Meier's *Civilization*.
* **Gameplay Connection:** Progress is not an uninterrupted tech ladder. Every technological thesis (e.g., intensive crop rotation, industrial scrip) generates its own material antithesis (soil exhaustion, peonage revolts), resolved only through systemic synthesis or collapse.

### 3. Economic Concepts Turned Subversive

#### 3.1 _The Invisible Fist_
* **Inspiration:** Adam Smith's *The Invisible Hand*.
* **Gameplay Connection:** Demonstrates that the "free market" requires armed constables, anti-enclosure crackdowns, truck acts, and sovereign bailouts to survive.
* **Tagline:** *"Where the invisible hand trembles, the state delivers the blow."*

#### 3.2 _The Metabolic Rift_
* **Inspiration:** Marx & John Bellamy Foster's theory of ecological rupture under capitalism.
* **Gameplay Connection:** Models the physical depletion of soil nutrients through urban extraction, reliance on guano imports, and the devastating Turnip Winter harvest collapse when trade lines snap.
* **Tagline:** *"Soil bleeds into capital. Capital bleeds into famine."*

#### 3.3 _The Cash Nexus_
* **Inspiration:** Thomas Carlyle / *The Communist Manifesto* (*"no other nexus between man and man than naked self-interest"*).
* **Gameplay Connection:** Direct tribute to the game's mathematical core: an airtight, zero-leak monetary economy where every coin and scrip voucher is conserved.

#### 3.4 _Corralito: Sovereign Contagion_
* **Inspiration:** Argentina's 2001 banking freeze (*El Corralito*) and Cyprus's 2013 depositor bail-in haircuts.
* **Gameplay Connection:** The horrifying moment a sovereign default spreads to domestic bank balance sheets, forcing the government to freeze ATMs, limit citizens to subsistence withdrawals, or haircut uninsured deposits.
