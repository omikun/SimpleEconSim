# Nile repair playability checkpoint

Run `MPLCONFIGDIR=/private/tmp/regnum-mpl venv/bin/python audit_egypt_food_project.py output.json`. The two paths fund a Nile Delta channel repair on turn 1, retain exports on turn 2, and answer the Caisse's payment windows. The second also requisitions private food on turn 5 and releases public meals on turn 7. They are seeded examples; repeated worlds in one process are not yet identical, so differences between their final totals are **not** a controlled estimate of either action's effect.

The repair transfers 45 treasury units to three local workers, needs two staffed turns, and then pays two units per turn in upkeep. It modifies the actual food-yield calculation in the named district. In one replay the turn-4 low-Nile harvest used a 1.80 yield factor, produced 120 food units, and 18 of 131 local households were underfed. The private warehouse held 60 units after buying from producers. The owner's purchase and its cash transfer are logged; additional production did not automatically reach poorer households.

The access path uses the existing requisition and release decisions to move real private food to public stores and then reserve meals for underfed households. This costs two more major decisions and can provoke owner and creditor reactions. In the captured runs both routes settled the Caisse claim but ended in fiscal hardship, with very high nationwide hunger. The repair is therefore an opening move in a distribution and labor contest, not a standalone food-security victory. Late reports also show large strikes and weak harvests in the Delta. Phase 5 must make that emerging threat visible; Phase 6 must balance multiple starting conditions and test whether a sustained food-first strategy can succeed.

The focused tests verify the action budget, conserved wage transfers, construction and upkeep, an actual 1.80 food-yield factor during the low-Nile shock, owner storage reporting, and a complete campaign that includes the repair. The presentation tests verify that existing Guided modals and decision rendering still work. A blind human playtest remains outstanding.

The full `test_scenario_playthroughs.py` run is failing outside this focused path. Several older scripted routes try to pay a 200-unit instalment with insufficient treasury cash; others fail to reach their declared endings. This is a campaign balance and route-reachability defect to address in Phase 6, not evidence that the food repair is balanced.
