Structural transporter, specialized traders, trader audits & exit liquidation

- New transporter.py Route: FIFO cargo queue per region-pair, governs
  delivery time with congestion (delay grows with queue depth). Goods
  move exporter -> route -> inventory_foreign; nothing is created or
  destroyed.
- Traders specialize: 3 per good (food/wood/furniture) via trade_good field.
- Arbitrage gate now includes transport cost: net foreign price must beat
  home price plus shipping cost by at least the 5% margin.
- foreign_sell renamed settle_trade: imports sell in the destination auction;
  settlement logs sales, feeds away-traders, posts FX asks.
- check_trader_holdings per-turn sanity audit flags positions that cannot
  clear a profit (bounded summaries; price-drift stranding expected, evicted
  at the 20-turn opportunity-cost benchmark).
- _exit_trader liquidation: reclaims route cargo, sells lots at 0.7x cost to
  other traders or escheats to gov inventory, pays down loans (cash down,
  liability down), residual debt stays with the ex-trader.
- Regions now 200 agents with 3 traders per good; wealth_diagnostic and
  wealth_lineage updated to match.
- One-off/temp scripts moved to tmp/.
