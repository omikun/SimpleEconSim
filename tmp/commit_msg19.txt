M2 conservation fixes: _buy negative-cash mint + PayDepositInterest cap

The M1 gate (behavior_drift.py, 3 seeds x 300t in one process) exposed two
latent pre-M2 conservation bugs once the unrest ladder changed the economy's
trajectory:

1. Region._buy: a.cash = max(0.0, a.cash - cash) erased pre-existing
   negative agent cash (debt) whenever bought==0. Seven agents at T=243
   carried negative balances summing to exactly 6.6553 C — the gate's
   "CURRENCY 'C' SUPPLY SHIFT $6.66". Cash now moves only when a purchase
   actually happened (bought > 0); debt stays on the books and is serviced
   by the loan/wage machinery.

2. Bank.PayDepositInterest: the payout cap only bounded against loan
   interest, not the deposit pool. When deposits were thin, interest
   payouts drove total_deposits negative (minting currency on the audit:
   home bank equity = deposits - liabilities). Payout is now also capped to
   max(0, total_deposits).
