Milestone D: InstitutionBundle — zero-behavior extraction for the province model

=== WHY ===
The engine's core invariant is "one tile == one full institution set" (its
own bank / government / charity).  That is load-bearing in three places:
 1. Region.__init__ builds self.bank/.gov/.charity per tile,
 2. Region.step() interleaves the per-tile economy with institutional flows
    (charity collect/distribute mid-auction, gov tax/food-buy/borrow, bank
    deposit interest) once per tile,
 3. forex.audit_currency_total sums bank.equity/fx_pool and charity cash PER
    TILE — sharing one bank across tiles without dedup double-counts it.

=== WHAT ===
- province.py (NEW): InstitutionBundle holder (bank/government/charity) +
  make_bundle() factory.  Wilderness => all three None (no institutions).
- region.py: Region.bank/.gov/.charity are now forwarding PROPERTIES into
  self._institutions (a bundle per tile in the legacy 1:1 layout).  All ~40
  existing read sites (region.py, world_trade.py, forex.py, econsim_live.py,
  claims.py, viewers, drivers) compile unchanged.  Setters route claims.py /
  construction writes into the bundle.  Region.step() is UNTOUCHED —
  byte-identical behavior this milestone.
