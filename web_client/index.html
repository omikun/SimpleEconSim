👑 REGNUM Player Guide & Mechanics

Macroeconomic Systems

• Conserved Money: Pure closed-loop economy. Money moves strictly through wages, trade, tax, bonds, and contracts.

• Surplus Value & Exploitation (s/v): Produced value minus subsistence wages. High exploitation spurs wildcat strikes and revolutionary unrest.

• Civil Unrest Stages: Calm → Unrest → Protest → Mob/Riot → Compromise → Takeover.
